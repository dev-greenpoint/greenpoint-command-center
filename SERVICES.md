# Full Campaign Breakdown by Service

## 1. PR

**Purpose:** Build earned media awareness, credibility and momentum around a brand, project, launch, milestone or thought leadership angle.

### What needs to be done:

**Strategy**
- Confirm campaign objective, audience and key messages.
- Identify media angles: business, trade, lifestyle, local, design, property, founder/profile, milestone.
- Review newsroom insights and current media appetite.
- Confirm spokespeople, proof points, risks and approvals.

**Materials**
- Draft press release or media alert.
- Develop media pitch copy.
- Prepare media kit: imagery, renders, logos, boilerplate, fact sheet, spokesperson bio.
- Confirm quote approvals.

**Media list**
- Build targeted pitch list by media category.
- Segment top-tier, trade, local, broadcast, digital and newsletter targets.
- Confirm any exclusives or embargo strategy.

**Execution**
- Pitch priority journalists first.
- Send wider pitch release.
- Conduct follow-ups.
- Manage media enquiries, interviews, image requests and fact checks.

**Monitoring & reporting**
- Track all coverage.
- Log reach, links, sentiment, key message inclusion and AVE where required.
- Share wins in Slack/client email.
- Include results in monthly or quarterly report.

**Core deliverables:** PR strategy, press release/media alert, pitch list, pitch email, media kit, coverage tracker, reporting summary.

## 2. PR Light

**Purpose:** A leaner PR option for lower-touch campaigns, smaller announcements or opportunistic media activity.

### What needs to be done:

**Strategy**
- Confirm the single strongest angle.
- Define realistic media targets.
- Keep scope tight: usually one announcement, one pitch angle, one outreach round.

**Materials**
- Draft short release, pitch note or media blurb.
- Prepare basic supporting assets.
- Confirm client approval.

**Media outreach**
- Build a focused list of high-relevance contacts.
- Pitch selected journalists.
- Complete one follow-up round.

**Reporting**
- Track coverage and journalist feedback.
- Provide a short wrap-up with outcomes and recommendations.

**Core deliverables:** Short release or pitch, focused media list, one outreach round, simple wrap-up.

> **Watch-out:** PR Light should not become full PR by stealth. Define limits upfront: number of angles, number of contacts, follow-up rounds and reporting depth.

## 3. Content - Social

**Purpose:** Build consistent brand visibility and engagement across social platforms.

### What needs to be done:

**Strategy**
- Confirm platforms, audience, tone, content pillars and posting cadence.
- Review campaign objectives and priority messages.
- Align with PR, events, influencer and paid activity.

**Planning**
- Gather client inputs, milestones, product/project updates, FAQs and upcoming moments.
- Create monthly or fortnightly content calendar.
- Map content across posts, reels, stories, LinkedIn updates and campaign moments.
- Brief design/copy support as needed.

**Production**
- Draft captions.
- Create or brief visuals.
- Source imagery/video.
- Prepare hashtags, tags, links and CTAs.
- Format platform-specific versions.

**Approvals & scheduling**
- Internal review by Social Media Manager/Account Manager.
- Client approval.
- Schedule via Sked or agreed tool.
- Check live posts after publishing.

**Community management**
- Monitor comments, DMs and engagement.
- Escalate sensitive comments or enquiries.
- Track frequently asked questions.

**Reporting**
- Report on reach, engagement, follower growth, clicks, saves, shares and best-performing content.
- Provide learnings and optimisation actions.

**Core deliverables:** Content calendar, captions, designed assets, scheduled posts, community management, monthly report.

---

## 4. Content - eDM

**Purpose:** Use email to drive direct communication, nurture leads, announce updates or support campaign conversion.

### What needs to be done:

**Strategy**
- Confirm audience segment, objective and CTA.
- Define send purpose: launch, update, invitation, nurture, announcement, sales push, event follow-up.
- Confirm database ownership and send platform.

**Content**
- Draft subject line options.
- Draft preview text.
- Write email body copy.
- Build CTA hierarchy.
- Prepare imagery, links and disclaimers.

**Build**
- Design or format eDM in the agreed platform.
- Add links, UTM tracking and buttons.
- Test desktop and mobile layout.
- Send test email internally and to client.

**Approval & send**
- Internal proof.
- Client approval.
- Final link check.
- Schedule/send.

**Reporting**
- Track open rate, click rate, unsubscribes, bounces and conversions where available.
- Capture learnings for next send.

**Core deliverables:** eDM copy, subject lines, design/build, test send, final send, performance summary.

---

## 5. Content - Web / Blogs / Other Copy

**Purpose:** Create longer-form or evergreen content that supports SEO, brand storytelling, lead nurture and campaign education.

### What needs to be done:

**Strategy**
- Confirm content type: blog, website copy, landing page, case study, FAQ, bio, brochure copy, advertorial-style content.
- Define audience, objective and key message.
- Identify SEO keywords where relevant.
- Confirm brand voice and required proof points.

**Research**
- Review source materials, campaign strategy, client notes and competitor context.
- Gather quotes, data, product/project details and links.

**Drafting**
- Write headline, intro, body copy and CTA.
- Structure for readability and conversion.
- Include internal links, external links and metadata if required.

**Review**
- Internal edit.
- Account Manager review.
- Client review.
- Final proof.

**Publishing handover**
- Provide final copy in upload-ready format.
- Include SEO title, meta description, image alt text and URL slug where needed.
- Confirm publishing responsibility.

**Reporting**
- Track traffic, dwell time, clicks, enquiries or content usage.

**Core deliverables:** Blog/article/copy draft, SEO recommendations, final approved copy, publishing notes.

---

## 6. Content - Influencer

**Purpose:** Drive awareness, credibility, content creation and social proof through creator partnerships.

### What needs to be done:

**Strategy**
- Confirm campaign objective: awareness, visitation, sales, content capture, brand credibility, event attendance.
- Define target audience and ideal creator profile.
- Confirm budget, gifted/paid model and deliverables.

**Influencer list**
- Research creators.
- Review audience fit, content quality, engagement, location, brand safety and previous partnerships.
- Prepare shortlist for client approval.

**Briefing**
- Create influencer brief.
- Include campaign context, key messages, deliverables, usage rights, timings, hashtags, tags, mandatory inclusions and approval process.
- Confirm disclosure requirements.

**Outreach & negotiation**
- Contact creators.
- Negotiate rates, inclusions and dates.
- Confirm contracts or written acceptance.
- Manage product/event/site logistics.

**Delivery**
- Share brief and assets.
- Track content deadlines.
- Review draft content if approvals are included.
- Monitor live posts and capture links/screenshots.

**Reporting**
- Capture reach, impressions, engagement, link clicks, content examples and qualitative insights.
- Summarise best-performing creators and future recommendations.

**Core deliverables:** Influencer strategy, influencer list, influencer brief, outreach tracker, booked creators, live content tracker, campaign report.

## 7. Content - Shortform Capture

**Purpose:** Capture lightweight social-first content for reels, TikTok, stories, LinkedIn, events or campaign updates.

### What needs to be done:

**Pre-production**
- Confirm shoot objective and required outputs.
- Build shot list.
- Confirm location, date, talent, access and run sheet.
- Confirm style: polished, lo-fi, behind-the-scenes, interview, walkthrough, event recap.

**Capture**
- Film vertical-first content.
- Capture hooks, transitions, B-roll, people, details, signage, product/project moments.
- Record short interviews or voice grabs if needed.
- Capture stills if included.

**Post-production**
- Select best clips.
- Edit into agreed number of reels/shorts/stories.
- Add captions, supers, music, branding and end cards.
- Export platform-ready files.

**Approval & scheduling**
- Internal review.
- Client approval.
- Schedule or hand over final files.

**Reporting**
- Track performance by format, hook, topic and platform.

**Core deliverables:** Shot list, shoot run sheet, raw footage folder, edited shortform assets, captions, scheduling notes.

---

## 8. Content - Video Production

**Purpose:** Produce higher-quality video assets for campaigns, websites, social, sales, investor relations, brand storytelling or paid media.

### What needs to be done:

**Strategy & creative**
- Confirm objective, audience, distribution channels and video type.
- Develop creative concept.
- Confirm format: hero video, interview, case study, brand film, campaign launch, event recap, paid ad cutdowns.
- Define required deliverables and specs.

**Pre-production**
- Script or talking points.
- Storyboard or shot list.
- Talent coordination.
- Location scouting.
- Production schedule.
- Permits, access, releases and run sheet.
- Crew booking.

**Production**
- Shoot video.
- Capture audio, B-roll, interviews and stills if needed.
- Manage client/talent on the day.

**Post-production**
- First edit.
- Client review.
- Revisions.
- Colour, sound, captions, supers, logos and end cards.
- Export all required formats.

**Distribution**
- Provide files for social, web, paid, eDM or presentations.
- Create cutdowns where required.

**Reporting**
- Track video views, completion rate, engagement, click-throughs and platform performance.

**Core deliverables:** Creative concept, script, shot list, production schedule, final video, cutdowns, captions, usage notes.

---

## 9. Brand Activation - Events & Partnerships

**Purpose:** Create real-world engagement, media moments, partnerships and community touchpoints.

### What needs to be done:

**Strategy**
- Define activation objective: awareness, sales, community engagement, launch moment, VIP experience, content creation, media attendance.
- Confirm target audience, budget and success metrics.
- Decide whether activity is event-led, partnership-led or both.

**Concept development**
- Develop creative idea.
- Create activation/event plan.
- Identify venue, partners, suppliers and content opportunities.
- Map guest journey.

**Budget & logistics**
- Build budget.
- Source quotes.
- Confirm suppliers.
- Manage contracts, invoices and approvals.
- Prepare run sheet, risk checklist and wet weather plan if needed.

**Invitations & promotion**
- Build invite list.
- Draft invitation copy.
- Design invite assets.
- Send invitations and track RSVPs.
- Coordinate media/influencer attendance where relevant.

**Delivery**
- Manage bump-in, event day and bump-out.
- Coordinate suppliers, client, guests, media and influencers.
- Capture content.
- Track attendance and issues.

**Post-event**
- Thank you emails.
- Coverage/content follow-up.
- Supplier wrap-up.
- Report results and recommendations.

**Core deliverables:** Activation concept, event plan, budget, supplier tracker, run sheet, invite list, RSVP tracker, event-day management, post-event report.

---

## 10. Awards

**Purpose:** Build credibility through relevant industry awards, business awards and project recognition.

### What needs to be done:

**Research**
- Identify relevant awards by sector, category, deadline, entry fee and judging criteria.
- Assess likelihood of success.
- Recommend priority awards calendar.

**Entry planning**
- Confirm project/brand/person being submitted.
- Gather required information: data, outcomes, testimonials, imagery, plans, media coverage, sustainability credentials, case studies.
- Map evidence against judging criteria.

**Writing**
- Draft award submission.
- Write executive/project summary.
- Develop supporting statements.
- Prepare proof points and measurable outcomes.

**Assets**
- Source imagery, renders, logos, videos, supporting documents.
- Check formatting and file requirements.

**Approval & submission**
- Internal proof.
- Client approval.
- Upload entry.
- Confirm payment and receipt.

**Amplification**
- If shortlisted/won, prepare social content, media pitch, website copy, LinkedIn post and stakeholder comms.

**Core deliverables:** Awards calendar, category recommendation, submission copy, supporting assets, final uploaded entry, amplification plan.

---

## 11. Paid Media

**Purpose:** Amplify campaign messages, drive traffic, leads, awareness or retargeting through paid channels.

### What needs to be done:

**Strategy**
- Confirm objective: awareness, traffic, leads, conversions, event RSVPs, database growth.
- Define audience segments.
- Confirm channels: Meta, LinkedIn, Google, publication paid placements, native, display, boosted social.
- Confirm budget, campaign dates and KPIs.

**Creative planning**
- Define ad formats.
- Write ad copy variations.
- Brief design/video assets.
- Prepare landing page or lead form requirements.

**Setup**
- Confirm ad account access.
- Install or check tracking pixels/UTMs.
- Build campaigns, ad sets and ads.
- Set targeting, budget and schedule.

**Approval**
- Internal review.
- Client approval of copy, creative, targeting and budget.
- Final pre-launch check.

**Live management**
- Launch campaign.
- Monitor performance.
- Optimise creative, targeting, budget allocation and placements.
- Pause underperforming ads.

**Reporting**
- Report spend, reach, impressions, CPM, CTR, CPC, leads, conversions and recommendations.

**Core deliverables:** Paid media plan, ad copy, creative brief/assets, campaign setup, optimisation log, paid report.

> **Watch-out:** Paid media needs clear ownership. If Greenpoint is coordinating with an external paid agency, define who owns strategy, creative, setup, optimisation and reporting.

## 12. Design

**Purpose:** Create campaign visuals and branded assets that support PR, content, events, social, paid, eDM and presentations.

### What needs to be done:

**Briefing**
- Confirm asset purpose, format, audience and platform.
- Gather brand guidelines, references, copy, image assets and specs.
- Confirm due dates and approval pathway.

**Asset planning**
- Define required outputs: social tiles, carousels, eDM headers, event invites, media kits, presentation decks, ads, signage, website banners.
- Confirm dimensions and file formats.

**Design**
- Create first draft.
- Check brand consistency.
- Ensure accessibility/readability.
- Prepare variations if needed.

**Review**
- Internal review.
- Copy proof.
- Client review.
- Revisions.

**Final delivery**
- Export final files.
- Save working files and final files in the correct folder.
- Provide platform-ready versions.

**Core deliverables:** Design brief, draft assets, final exported files, editable source files, asset folder.
